### Deposit Bonus FAQs

### How do I receive the Bonus?

Sign up for our Live Account, deposit and activate the bonus in your client area.

### Can I withdraw the Deposit bonus?

No, the bonus is non-withdrawable. However, all profits earned from trades using the bonus are yours to keep.

### What happens if I make a partial or full withdrawal?

Partial or full withdrawals will result in a proportional removal of the bonus credit, based on the amount withdrawn.

### Are there any restrictions on how I can use the bonus?

Yes, the bonus cannot be used for hedging strategies. Also, it cannot be used for internal transfers, rebates, or adjustments.

### Am I eligible it?

Eligibility is based on your account's jurisdiction, risk tier, and the successful completion of the application process.

The bonus is not available to Fund Managers or clients using MAM or PAMM accounts.

Clients who violate our trading terms, engage in fraudulent activities, or provide misleading information may lose their eligibility.

### What are the risks of using Deposit Bonus?

While our Deposit Bonus amplifies your trading power, it also increases risk. Leverage can magnify both gains and losses, so it's vital to manage your exposure carefully.
