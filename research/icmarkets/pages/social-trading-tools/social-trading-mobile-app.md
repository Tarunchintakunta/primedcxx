### FAQs

##### About IC Social

##### What is IC Social?

IC Social is a social trading app that allows traders to replicate/copy the strategies of signal providers and also to become a signal provider by using your IC MetaTrader 4/MetaTrader 5 accounts.

##### How do I become IC Social user?

You can download the IC Social app from Google Play store or iOS app store. If you are an existing client of IC and hold a live accounts, you can sign up with your registered email address. If you are not an IC client yet, you can apply for a Live Account , once approved, you can connect your MetaTrader 4 account to IC Social.

##### Account settings

##### How do I connect / link my trading account to the app?

You can Link your IC account to IC Social by entering your account login details within the IC Social app.

##### I cannot connect my account

There maybe multiple reasons why your account cannot connect. Please make sure you are doing the following:

- You are connecting with your TRADING password not your INVESTOR account.
- You're entering the wrong server, account number or password.
- You are a Signal account and you need to close all trades from an existing account you had connected.
- Your MetaTrader 4 password is too long - it needs to be less than 15 characters.

##### How do I unlink my trading account?

If you are a strategy provider , you will have to sent your request via email at support@pelicantrading.io .

If you are registered as a copier, navigate to 'Account' , 'Settings' , 'Trading account' and click the red "X Button" - the top right of the screen. Please note that you will be automatically redirected to the Discover section.

##### How do I switch trading account?

Navigate to 'Account' , select 'Link account' and input your new trading account details.

##### How can I change my account details?

Select 'Account' , 'Settings' and then 'Edit profile' . In this page, you will be able to change your name, biography, website and location.

##### Copying

##### How do I copy a trader?

To copy a trader, select the Account you would like to copy, select Copy, you may have to complete a Suitability Form, once you have completed the Form, select your copy size:Proportional by Equity (trade sizes are proportional to the copiers equity relative to the Signal)

Mirror Master Size (trade size is the same as the Signal irrespective of the equity on account)

Fixed Size (locks the size of trades for all trades)

Multiplier (adjusts your copied trade size based on your selected value (0.1–10), scaling it relative to the Strategy Provider’s trades under various settings, with 1x keeping the size unchanged.)

Select whether you want to copy already open trades that the Signal has open, and finally select whether you want trades to be rounded to the minimum trade size - even if we have calculated a copy size smaller than your broker has available.

Press 'Confirm'.

##### What does proportional by equity mean?

When copying a signal, 'Proportional by equity' adjusts the size of your trade relative to your account size so it mirrors the size of the signal's trade in proportion to their account size. This means you are taking a similar risk to the signal you are copying. Buying $5,000 of CAP40 is much more risky for an account with a balance of $5,500 than for one with $100,000!

##### Can I trade while my account is connected to a Signal?

Yes, you can place trades manually while an account is connected to a Signal provider. You have full control and also full responsibility of the actions taken / allowed on your account

##### Signal & Strategy

##### What is a signal?

A signal is an account that you are copying. Signals can also be referred to as "Strategies" or a "Fund". In your 'Account' section you will have a list of Signals. The list identifies the different accounts you have chosen to copy.

##### How to become a Signal provider?

Anyone can be a signal provider and can select that option when you link your account. Most signals have at least 6 months of positive trading experience and a minimum balance of 500 USD. Once you linked your live trade account to the app, you can then select to be Signal Provider. You will be required to fill the form and assessment questionnaire.

##### How to set a performance fee?

Signals can set a Performance Fee as soon as they are approved . Please head to the 'Account' page and choose to 'set fee' and then 'performance' . This can be as much as 50% and can be changed at any point. If you have been copying a free signal that later begins charging a performance fee, the fee will only apply to profits earned from the moment you re-copy and accept the new terms — not to any previous profits. For example, if a signal changes from 0% to 20% performance fee, and you re-copy under those terms, the 20% fee will apply only to new profits going forward.

##### When is a performance fee charged?

Performance fees are calculated based on your Broker's preferred interval: Instantly, Daily , Weekly or Monthly. Fees are then deducted shortly thereafter when they have been processed. They will be labelled clearly in your account. IC offers daily performance fee payout for Signal providers.

##### How is a Performance Fee calculated?

Performance Fees is calculated automatically on the profitable trades only. If the copier is registered with IC you receive the fees the same day, while all fees from external Brokers are collected at the beginning of each month and will be paid from them usually the first week of the month.

##### Max DrawDown/Risk

##### What does 'Mirror Master Size' mean?

Mirror master size copies the size of the signal that you wish to copy. For example, if the trader you wish to copy has bought $50 worth of bitcoin, choosing "mirror master size" will buy you $50 worth of Bitcoin. This function copies the signal trade size for all trades, irrespective your or the trader's account size.
